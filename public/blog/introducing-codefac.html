Introducing Codefac

Jun 26, 2026

Originally written for the Codefac blog. Check out Codefac.

Codefac is for the work that starts messy: a Slack thread, a customer note, a half-formed bug report, a product instinct that has not yet become a ticket. Most teams already know what needs to move forward, but the handoff from intent to implementation is where momentum leaks. Codefac turns those signals into agent pipelines that can investigate, plan, write, test, and prepare changes in isolated sandboxes, while keeping people close to the decisions that matter.

We are building it around a simple belief: software should feel less like pushing tasks through a queue and more like working with a capable workshop that understands your codebase, your tools, and your taste. Agents can do more than generate snippets when they are given the right runtime, context, permissions, and review gates. Codefac is that operating layer: a place where product judgment, engineering standards, and automation meet so teams can move from signal to shipped work with less ceremony and more trust.
